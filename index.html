FAQs

Questions, answered.

Here are some useful details before you get started.

How much does a website cost?

A simple starter website begins at Rs 1,000. Larger projects are quoted after discussing the pages, design and functionality. The final scope and price are confirmed before work begins.

How do I pay the advance?

The current Easypaisa payment number is 03152311498. Confirm the recipient and payment instructions before sending money. The advance is deducted from the agreed total price.

What is the advance policy?

The first order requires Rs 300 in advance. For later orders, the minimum advance is Rs 500. Any different arrangement must be agreed upon before work starts.

Can you make my website mobile-friendly?

Responsive design is included in the packages where specified. We confirm the exact deliverables for your selected project before starting.

Can you publish the website online?

We can discuss deployment options, including Cloudflare Pages for suitable static websites. Domain registration, paid services and other third-party charges are separate unless expressly included.

When will my website be ready?

Timing depends on the scope, content, required features and feedback. We'll discuss a realistic timeline before accepting the project.
